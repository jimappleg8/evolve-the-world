# ✍️ Sign the Cultural Creator’s Pledge

The ability to sign the Cultural Creator's pledge is currently being developed. 

Thank you for you patience.

{#

This pledge is designed to both help you understand what we are trying to do and to help you make a positive move toward joining the community of cultural creators.

The idea is that if you can honestly answer these questions with the indicated answers, you are in the right place.

- **Do you believe all humans are inherently equal?** Answer yes.
- **Do you love all life and seek wellbeing for all?** Answer yes.
- **Do you find it morally acceptable that some people gain at the expense of others?** Answer no.
- **Do you love truth for truth’s sake?** Answer yes.
